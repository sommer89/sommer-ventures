What you'll be doing

Start in the non-league.

Your first club has more problems than money, and it sits at the bottom of the pyramid. Win promotions one division at a time, win cups along the way, and turn a tiny club into a household name. The league does not wait for you to be ready.

Watch it happen, minute by minute.

Follow the big games as they unfold, with commentary, goals, substitutions and cards as they land — or speed it up and skip straight to the result. Pick the formation, pick the eleven, and find out whether it works.

Put the squad together.

Bid for stars, pick up free transfers and assemble a side that wins the league. The transfer market moves on without you and rivals chase the same targets, so act first. Set the starting eleven by list or by formation, and keep an eye on how the squad fits together.

Grow your own.

Bring teenagers into the academy and develop them into first-team players instead of buying them. Watch the attributes tick up month by month across the senior squad, the youth side and the academy — and watch a player you raised get called up for his country.

Hire the gaffer. Sack him if you must.

You are not the manager — you are the person who appoints him. Judge him on tactics, man-management and discipline, extend his contract when it is going well, and dismiss him when it is not. He knows when you have been looking at replacements.

Build the backroom.

Choose the assistant manager, the coaches and the scouts. A good physio keeps the squad fit and a good scout finds the gems before anyone else does. Your number of seats grows as you climb, and specialist coaches unlock further up the pyramid.

Know every player.

Every player has a value, a wage, a contract and a history. Negotiate new terms, list him for transfer or loan, and follow his season and career appearances, goals and ratings — with a progression curve that shows exactly how far he has come.

Also in the game

A living football world

Thousands of players across a hundred countries. Every nation's clubs field real squads that develop, move and age while you build.

European nights

Qualify for continental competition and take on the best clubs abroad — group stages, two-legged knockouts and a trophy at the end.

International football

Develop a teenager into a star and watch him get called up. Follow your players through international tournaments while your season rolls on.

The books

Set ticket prices, grow your income, upgrade the facilities and stay out of debt. The money you make now buys the squad you want later.

The supporters

Your fans have a mood. Win and the crowds grow; charge too much or drift, and they let you know. Derby days and big finals bring the noise.

Play it your way

Five minutes on the bus or a full season in one sitting. It all fits on your phone, and no two clubs turn out the same.
