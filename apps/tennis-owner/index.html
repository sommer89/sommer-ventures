Tennis Owner

Build an academy. Make champions. You own a tennis academy in the country you choose, starting at the lowest tier with one pro. Sign players, hire coaches, build courts and facilities, plan schedules, coach your best player point by point, and grow juniors into champions on the men's and women's tours.

Free with optional in-app purchases · iPhone · iOS 17 or later · No account, no sign-up.

Privacy

There is no account and no sign-up, and your academy saves straight to your device. There are no trackers. The game can show a rewarded video ad from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one, and in-app purchases are optional and handled by Apple. The details are in the Tennis Owner privacy policy, and the rules of use are in the terms of use.
